The standard

Our origination platform partner maintains SOC 2 Type II audited controls for bank-level data security.

SOC 2 Type II certification is held by the platform provider. GenerEdge, Inc. does not itself claim SOC 2 certification.

National reach, hyper-local delivery

While GenerEdge operates within a national network, reaching minority-owned (MBE) and women-owned (WBE) businesses across all 50 states, we specialize in hyper-local delivery. Our platform uses a national lender waterfall to deliver high-intent business loan requests from business owners exactly within a lender's field of membership.
