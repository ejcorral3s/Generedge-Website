About us

Where technology meets advocacy

We connect business borrowers with a network of credit unions, CDFIs, and community lenders that offer rates and terms aligned with each borrower's needs, without regard to their race or gender.

Schedule a meeting

Built on a fintech backbone

We offer an AI-assisted loan origination platform to a growing network of lenders who are actively seeking to deploy capital to women and minority entrepreneurs.

Our fintech backbone

Partnership with Finturf, a Lending-as-a-Service platform

The intelligence

SafeTurf.ai 360°, an AI-driven KYB/KYC and fraud monitoring tool

The standard

Our origination platform partner maintains SOC 2 Type II audited controls for bank-level data security.

SOC 2 Type II certification is held by the platform provider. GenerEdge, Inc. does not itself claim SOC 2 certification.

National reach, hyper-local delivery

While GenerEdge operates within a national network, reaching minority-owned (MBE) and women-owned (WBE) businesses across all 50 states, we specialize in hyper-local delivery. Our platform uses a national lender waterfall to deliver high-intent business loan requests from business owners exactly within a lender's field of membership.
